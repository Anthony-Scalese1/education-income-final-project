# education-income-final-project
This is my final project for my Applied Statistics for Social Scientists course. In this project, I analyzed various data across all fifty states, relating to weather, size, demographics, education, and income. I decided to determine whether or not there is a positive correlation between bachelor's degrees and income in the US.
# Instructions
To access and utilize the script, open the "scalesa-final_project.R" file, which contains the code for my findings. To access the dataset, open the "Table Template Final Project - Codebook.csv" file. 
# Data
The data used for the analyses was gathered across all fifty states and surveys many different topics, including but not limited to, weather and its effects, population, size, education level, agriculture, income, and education. I gathered different data regarding education, however, for the project, I chose to focus on the percentage of a state's population in possession of bachelor's degrees. Additionally, I found the median income of households across the nation between 2020 and 2024.

I gathered the data by looking at various government websites and finding the value that correlates with each state. To find information about awarded bachelor's degrees, I used the United States Census Bureau's website. To find information about household income, I went to the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis' website.
