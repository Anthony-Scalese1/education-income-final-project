# education-income-final-project
This is my final project for my Applied Statistics for Social Scientists course. In this project, I analyzed various data across all fifty states, relating to weather, size, demographics, education, and income. I decided to determine whether or not there is a positive correlation between bachelor's degrees and income in the US.
# Instructions
To access and utilize the script, open the "scalesa-final_project.R" file, which contains the code for my findings. To access the dataset, open the "Table Template Final Project - Codebook.csv" file. 
# Data
The data used for the analyses was gathered across all fifty states and surveys many different topics, including but not limited to, weather and its effects, population, size, education level, agriculture, income, and education. I gathered different data regarding education, however, for the project, I chose to focus on the percentage of a state's population in possession of bachelor's degrees. Additionally, I found the median income of households across the nation in 2025

I gathered the data by looking at various government websites and finding the value that correlates with each state. To find information about awarded bachelor's degrees, I used the United States Census Bureau's website. To find information about household income, I went to the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis' website.
# Operationalization
The concept of education was operationalized by finding the percentage of people in each state that hold at least at bachelor's degree. Some states such as Massachusetts have very high percentages while others, such as West Virginia have very low percentages in comparison to the national average. Because the data is shown in percentages, it is relative to the population of the state, as to not skew toward larger states.

The concept of income was operationalized by finding the median income of households across the US. By using the median rather than the mean, the data is not skewed by any outliers and is an accurate representation of the entire population.
